clips_stud

clips_stud is a personal tool that cuts my own videos into short vertical clips and publishes them to my own TikTok account. It is used only by its developer.

How it works: I log in with my TikTok account (Login Kit), review a finished clip, and press "Post". The clip is then uploaded to my account through the TikTok Content Posting API. Nothing is posted without my explicit action.
