Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. What data is used

When the developer connects a TikTok account, the app receives an access token and basic profile information (such as the display name) through TikTok Login Kit. Videos are uploaded to TikTok only when the developer chooses to post them.

2. Where data is stored

The access token and videos are stored only on the developer's own computer. No data is collected from other people.

3. Sharing

Data is not sold, shared, or transferred to third parties, except to TikTok when a video is posted by the developer.

4. Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time in TikTok settings (Security and permissions, Apps and services). Stored data can be deleted by removing the app's files from the computer.
